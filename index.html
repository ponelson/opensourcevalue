Everyone builds on what is free. Someone else often keeps the value.

This is a map of how that happens: both how open foundations rose into monopolies, and how incumbents seeded open source so others would build (and pay) on top.

The mechanism

Commoditize your complement

A profit-maximizing company wants the things it sells to be expensive and the things it depends on to be cheap. Open source is the most efficient way ever invented to drive the price of a complement to zero, which is exactly why the largest companies fund so much of it.

Make the layer below you free, and the demand flows up to the scarce thing you still own.

Joel Spolsky framed it in 2002. The AI era is the largest test of it yet.
